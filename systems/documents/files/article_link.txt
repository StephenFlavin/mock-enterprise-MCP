NEWS ARTICLE REFERENCE
Allegation of Worker Exploitation at a Tamil Nadu Spinning Mill

Source: International Labour News Network (original reporting by its South Asia desk)
Published: about three weeks ago
Title: Debt Bondage Allegations at Tamil Nadu Spinning Mill

Summary:
The article alleges that Law Knitting Mill, a spinning and knitting mill in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, India,
recruits migrant workers through debt bondage. It says the mill sells yarn and knitted fabric to garment
factories that export to UK and European retailers, and names no retailer.

Key Allegations:
- Workers recruited through labour agents who advance recruitment fees, then deduct repayments from wages
- Interest on the advances is excessive and repayment terms impossible to meet
- Identity documents held by the mill, and movement restricted outside work hours
- Conditions described as meeting the ILO indicators of forced labour

Reporting Sources:
- Interviews with 14 current and former workers, anonymised
- Findings of a local NGO investigation (Southern Workers Rights Forum), which the journalist says was shared with her
- The mill declined to comment before publication

Credibility Notes:
- Worker testimony is consistent across the 14 interviews
- The NGO report is cited but not published; it should be requested directly
- ILO and other NGOs have documented debt bondage in Tamil Nadu spinning mills for several years
- No independent audit of the mill is cited

ESCALATION NEEDED:
Verify if this mill supplies our fabric mills or factories directly or indirectly.
Check audit records and supplier relationship data for connections.
