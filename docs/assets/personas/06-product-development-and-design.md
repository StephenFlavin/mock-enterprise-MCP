# 06 · Product Development and Design

Personas 51–60. Questions each role needs to answer to do their job.

**Question types:** `MONITOR` what is happening now · `DIAGNOSE` why it happened · `FORECAST` what will happen · `DECIDE` what to do · `RESEARCH` external or reference lookup

---

## 51. New product developer (NPD)
**Focus:** Take a concept through to launch on time, on cost and compliant.
**Tools/data:** PLM system, critical path/timeline tools, supplier quotes, sample and test status, cost sheets.

- `MONITOR` Where is each product against its critical path, and what is at risk of missing the launch date?
- `DECIDE` Which supplier and factory should develop this product given capability, cost and lead time?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why did the sample fail (design, material, supplier capability) and what changes are needed?
- `RESEARCH` What comparable products, materials and technologies exist in the market?
- `FORECAST` What will the unit cost, margin and minimum order be at expected volume?

## 52. Product designer
**Focus:** Create appealing, on-trend, manufacturable products.
**Tools/data:** Design software, trend reports, mood boards, material libraries, sales performance data.

- `RESEARCH` What are the key trends in style, colour, materials and function for the target customer and season?
- `MONITOR` What are our best and worst performers, and what design attributes explain it?
- `RESEARCH` What are competitors launching, and how do their designs, prices and claims compare?
- `DECIDE` Which design options best fit the brand, price point and sourcing constraints?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why do customers return or review badly for certain designs (fit, quality, appearance vs. photo)?

## 53. Technical designer / garment technologist
**Focus:** Turn designs into manufacturable specifications; manage fit and tolerances.
**Tools/data:** Tech packs, fit session notes, size charts, supplier feedback, test reports.

- `DIAGNOSE` Why does the sample not meet spec or fit (pattern, grading, fabric behaviour, factory interpretation)?
- `DECIDE` What adjustments to pattern, construction or tolerance will fix this?
- `MONITOR` Which styles have open fit or technical comments, and what is overdue?
- `RESEARCH` What size standards, grading practice and construction methods suit this product and market?
- `DIAGNOSE` Do return reasons and customer comments suggest a systemic fit problem in a size or style?

## 54. Product manager (own brand)
**Focus:** Own range performance, pipeline and lifecycle.
**Tools/data:** Sales, margin and stock reports, customer feedback, roadmap tools, competitor tracking.

- `MONITOR` How is each product performing on sales, margin, returns, ratings and stock?
- `DECIDE` Which products should we extend, refresh, price-change or delete?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why is a product underperforming (price, availability, quality, content, positioning)?
- `RESEARCH` What unmet customer needs, whitespace and competitor moves should shape the pipeline?
- `FORECAST` What is the likely sales and margin of next season's pipeline?

## 55. Packaging designer
**Focus:** Design packaging that sells, protects and complies at the lowest practical cost.
**Tools/data:** Dieline and design tools, packaging specs, test results, regulatory marks, supplier capabilities.

- `DECIDE` What format and material best balance protection, shelf appeal, freight efficiency and sustainability?
- `RESEARCH` What legal marks, recycling labels, warnings and language requirements apply in each destination?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why does this pack fail (drop test, crush, leakage, scuffing, customer complaints)?
- `FORECAST` What is the cost and pallet-fill impact of a packaging change?
- `RESEARCH` What are competitors and best-in-class retailers doing in packaging design and sustainability?

## 56. Trend / insight analyst
**Focus:** Turn market and consumer data into actionable trend insights.
**Tools/data:** Social listening, search trend data, market research subscriptions, sales data, survey tools.

- `RESEARCH` What emerging trends, themes and behaviours are growing and how fast?
- `DIAGNOSE` Is this trend a fad or a lasting shift, and for which customer segments and regions?
- `DECIDE` Which trends are worth acting on for our brand and what is the risk?
- `MONITOR` How do search, social and sales data correlate for key categories?
- `RESEARCH` What are competitors and adjacent industries doing with these trends?

## 57. Sample room coordinator
**Focus:** Track samples; keep approvals and fittings on schedule.
**Tools/data:** PLM sample tracker, courier tracking, approval workflows, sample inventory.

- `MONITOR` Where is each sample, who has it, and which are late or missing?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why was this sample delayed (supplier, customs, courier, internal approval)?
- `DECIDE` Which samples should be prioritised or couriered by air to meet the next review?
- `MONITOR` Which approvals are outstanding and blocking progress?
- `RESEARCH` What customs and shipping requirements apply for sending samples to this country?

## 58. Costing analyst
**Focus:** Build and challenge product costs; protect margin.
**Tools/data:** Costing templates, supplier quotes, bill of materials, FX and freight rates, duty rates.

- `DIAGNOSE` How does the quoted cost break down (material, labour, overhead, margin), and what looks high versus benchmark?
- `FORECAST` What is the landed cost under different volumes, origins, freight rates, FX and duty scenarios?
- `DECIDE` What design or material changes would hit the target cost with least customer impact?
- `MONITOR` How do actual costs compare with the costed price across seasons?
- `RESEARCH` What are current material, labour and freight rates in each origin?

## 59. Product data / PIM specialist
**Focus:** Keep product information complete, consistent and compliant across channels.
**Tools/data:** PIM, product attribute templates, supplier data feeds, content tools, channel feeds.

- `MONITOR` Which products have missing or inconsistent attributes, images or compliance data, and by channel?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why are listings rejected or incorrect on marketplaces and our website?
- `DECIDE` What attribute and taxonomy structure best supports search, filters and marketplaces?
- `RESEARCH` What data and content requirements do each marketplace and country have?
- `DIAGNOSE` Which data errors cause returns, complaints or lost sales?

## 60. R&D materials scientist
**Focus:** Develop and validate new and alternative materials.
**Tools/data:** Lab results, material databases, scientific literature, supplier technical data, pilot trials.

- `RESEARCH` What alternative materials exist with the needed performance, cost and lower footprint?
- `DECIDE` Which candidate material should progress to pilot and what tests are needed?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why did the material fail durability, safety or performance tests?
- `FORECAST` Can this material scale in supply and cost to our volumes?
- `RESEARCH` What patents, regulations and literature are relevant to the material?
