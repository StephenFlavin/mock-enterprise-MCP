# 04 · Procurement and Sourcing

Personas 32–41. Questions each role needs to answer to do their job.

**Question types:** `MONITOR` what is happening now · `DIAGNOSE` why it happened · `FORECAST` what will happen · `DECIDE` what to do · `RESEARCH` external or reference lookup

---

## 32. Category buyer
**Focus:** Choose suppliers, negotiate cost and terms, and shape the range.
**Tools/data:** Supplier quotes, cost breakdowns, sales and margin data, contracts, ERP/procurement platform.

- `DECIDE` Which supplier gives the best total landed cost, quality and reliability for this item?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why is this supplier's price higher than benchmark (materials, labour, margin, MOQ)?
- `RESEARCH` What are market prices and input cost trends for this category, and what do competitors pay or sell for?
- `MONITOR` How are my suppliers performing on cost, delivery, quality and compliance?
- `FORECAST` How will cost inflation, FX and tariffs affect next season's buying margin?

## 33. Sourcing manager (Asia)
**Focus:** Select and manage factories in Asia; control lead time and capacity.
**Tools/data:** Factory capability data, audit reports, production schedules, sample and bulk lead times, trade data.

- `DECIDE` Which factories have the capability, capacity and compliance record for this product?
- `MONITOR` Are production orders on schedule, and what is each factory's capacity utilisation?
- `DIAGNOSE` What is causing production delays (materials, labour, subcontracting, quality rework)?
- `RESEARCH` What are current labour costs, wage laws, export rules and political risks in each sourcing country?
- `DECIDE` Should we shift volume between countries to manage cost, tariffs or risk?

## 34. Sourcing manager (Europe/nearshore)
**Focus:** Develop faster, lower-risk supply closer to market.
**Tools/data:** Supplier databases, landed cost models, lead time data, trade agreements, trade fair and market intel.

- `DECIDE` Which products should move to nearshore sourcing based on speed, flexibility and total cost?
- `FORECAST` What would landed cost and lead time be if we reshored this product, compared with Asia?
- `RESEARCH` Which nearshore suppliers exist with the right capability, certifications and capacity?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why is nearshore cost higher or lower than expected (labour, material availability, duty, freight)?
- `MONITOR` How are nearshore suppliers performing on reaction time, quality and minimum order flexibility?

## 35. Supplier relationship manager
**Focus:** Build strategic supplier partnerships and drive joint improvement.
**Tools/data:** Supplier scorecards, business review packs, contracts, risk and performance data.

- `MONITOR` How is each strategic supplier performing against KPIs, and what is the trend?
- `DIAGNOSE` What underlying issues explain repeated misses (capacity, financial stress, quality system, our own forecast changes)?
- `DECIDE` Which suppliers should we invest in, develop, consolidate or exit?
- `RESEARCH` What are this supplier's financial health, ownership changes, news and public disputes?
- `FORECAST` What would a supplier failure or capacity loss do to our business, and what is the backup?

## 36. Procurement analyst
**Focus:** Analyse spend, find savings and track delivery of them.
**Tools/data:** Spend cube, invoices, contracts, PO data, savings tracker, BI tools.

- `MONITOR` Where is the money going by category, supplier, region and business unit?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why did spend rise or deviate from contract (maverick spend, price creep, volume, FX)?
- `DECIDE` Which categories offer the largest savings opportunities, and which levers apply (consolidation, renegotiation, specification change)?
- `MONITOR` How much of the planned saving has actually been realised and banked?
- `RESEARCH` What price benchmarks and index trends apply to our top categories?

## 37. Indirect procurement manager
**Focus:** Source non-merchandise goods and services such as packaging, IT, facilities and marketing.
**Tools/data:** Procurement platform, tenders and RFPs, contracts, service-level data.

- `DECIDE` Which supplier should win this tender, based on price, capability, risk and fit?
- `MONITOR` Which contracts are expiring in the next 6–12 months and what is the plan for each?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why is spend with this service provider above budget or above market?
- `RESEARCH` What alternatives and market benchmarks exist for this service or equipment?
- `FORECAST` What will energy, facilities and services costs be next year?

## 38. Contracts/legal counsel (commercial)
**Focus:** Draft and negotiate supplier terms; manage IP and liability.
**Tools/data:** Contract management system, template library, regulatory sources, supplier correspondence.

- `DIAGNOSE` What risks and unusual terms exist in this supplier contract compared with our standard?
- `DECIDE` Which clauses (liability, IP ownership, termination, price review, force majeure) should we push back on?
- `MONITOR` Which contract obligations, renewal dates and notice periods are approaching?
- `RESEARCH` What do current laws (supply chain due diligence, product liability, data, anti-bribery) require in the relevant jurisdictions?
- `DECIDE` What is our position and remedy if a supplier breaches delivery or quality terms?

## 39. Raw materials/commodities buyer
**Focus:** Buy key materials at good prices and manage price volatility.
**Tools/data:** Commodity price feeds, supplier quotes, hedging tools, material specs, market reports.

- `MONITOR` What are current prices and recent trends for cotton, resin, metals, leather, energy and other key inputs?
- `FORECAST` Where are prices likely to move in 3–12 months and what is the impact on product cost?
- `DECIDE` Should we lock in forward prices, hedge, or buy on the spot market now?
- `RESEARCH` What crop yields, supply disruptions, trade policies and demand shifts are affecting each material?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why did our cost differ from the market index (premiums, quality grade, logistics, contract lag)?

## 40. Packaging buyer
**Focus:** Source packaging that protects product, meets regulations and hits cost and sustainability goals.
**Tools/data:** Packaging specs, supplier quotes, damage and return data, regulatory and recycled-content rules.

- `DECIDE` Which packaging format is the lowest total cost considering material, freight density, damage and labour?
- `DIAGNOSE` Which packaging causes damage, returns or customer complaints?
- `RESEARCH` What are the current rules on recyclability, recycled content, plastic taxes and EPR fees by country?
- `FORECAST` What will packaging material prices and compliance costs be over the next 12–24 months?
- `MONITOR` Are we on track for our packaging sustainability targets?

## 41. Supplier onboarding specialist
**Focus:** Vet and set up new suppliers safely and quickly.
**Tools/data:** Onboarding workflow, KYC/due diligence databases, questionnaires, certificate checks, ERP vendor master.

- `MONITOR` Which onboarding cases are open, blocked or overdue, and what is missing?
- `DIAGNOSE` Are there red flags in this supplier's ownership, sanctions status, financials or adverse media?
- `DECIDE` Does this supplier meet our minimum requirements, or do we need conditions or further audit?
- `RESEARCH` What certifications, registrations and legal requirements apply to this supplier's country and category?
- `DECIDE` Is the bank, tax and contact data genuine and consistent (fraud check)?
