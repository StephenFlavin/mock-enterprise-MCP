# 08 · Commercial, Stores and E-commerce

Personas 71–80. Questions each role needs to answer to do their job.

**Question types:** `MONITOR` what is happening now · `DIAGNOSE` why it happened · `FORECAST` what will happen · `DECIDE` what to do · `RESEARCH` external or reference lookup

---

## 71. Store manager
**Focus:** Run a profitable store: availability, staffing, shrink and customer experience.
**Tools/data:** Store systems, sales and stock reports, rota tools, delivery schedules, shrink reports.

- `MONITOR` How are sales, conversion and availability tracking today versus target?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why do we have gaps on shelf when the system shows stock (back-room, mis-pick, delivery shortfall, stock error)?
- `DECIDE` How should I schedule and allocate staff to match footfall and deliveries?
- `DIAGNOSE` What is driving shrink in my store (theft, damage, process error, supplier shortages)?
- `RESEARCH` What are the promotions, product changes and compliance requirements arriving next week?

## 72. Store replenishment / back-room associate
**Focus:** Receive deliveries and keep shelves filled.
**Tools/data:** Handheld device, delivery manifests, store stock system, planograms.

- `MONITOR` What is arriving today, and which items are priority for shelves or promotions?
- `DIAGNOSE` The system says we have stock but I can't find it. Where could it be (back-room location, wrong department, in transit)?
- `DECIDE` Which products should go to shelf first, and what goes to overstock or back to the DC?
- `MONITOR` Which items are low or out on shelf now?
- `RESEARCH` Where does this product belong on the planogram, and what are the display requirements?

## 73. Regional / area manager
**Focus:** Lift performance across a cluster of stores.
**Tools/data:** Store performance dashboards, audit reports, labour and sales data, local market info.

- `MONITOR` Which stores are above or below target for sales, margin, availability, shrink and labour cost?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why is one store underperforming compared to similar stores (location, management, stock, competition)?
- `DECIDE` Where should I focus visits, investment, or stock rebalancing?
- `FORECAST` What will the region deliver for the period and what are the risks?
- `RESEARCH` What local competitor openings, events and economic conditions affect my stores?

## 74. E-commerce manager
**Focus:** Drive online sales and reliable fulfilment, especially at peak.
**Tools/data:** Web analytics, order management, fulfilment and stock feeds, promotions, customer service data.

- `MONITOR` How are traffic, conversion, basket size and fulfilment performance tracking by channel and device?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why did conversion or sales drop (stock availability, site fault, price, delivery promise, competitor)?
- `DECIDE` Which delivery promises, cut-off times and shipping options should we offer given DC and carrier capacity?
- `FORECAST` What peak order volume should we expect, and can the DC and carriers handle it?
- `RESEARCH` What are competitors offering on price, delivery and returns, and how do customers rate them?

## 75. Marketplace / wholesale account manager
**Focus:** Manage B2B customers and third-party marketplace listings.
**Tools/data:** Account CRM, EDI orders, marketplace seller dashboards, price lists, customer scorecards.

- `MONITOR` How are orders, fill rate and OTIF for each account, and what penalties are at risk?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why was this customer's order short or late, and what do we tell them?
- `DECIDE` How should limited stock be allocated across wholesale and marketplace accounts?
- `RESEARCH` What are the marketplace's current fee, policy and compliance changes?
- `FORECAST` What orders are likely from key accounts next quarter, given their sell-through?

## 76. Customer service agent
**Focus:** Resolve customer queries quickly: order status, complaints, refunds.
**Tools/data:** CRM/helpdesk, order and tracking systems, returns portal, policy knowledge base.

- `MONITOR` Where is this customer's order and what is the expected delivery date?
- `DIAGNOSE` What went wrong with this order (stock, carrier, address, payment) and what is the fastest fix?
- `DECIDE` What refund, replacement or goodwill applies under our policy?
- `MONITOR` What are the most common contact reasons today and are any spiking?
- `RESEARCH` What are the legal rights and policy rules for this product, country and situation?

## 77. Pricing analyst
**Focus:** Set and monitor prices to balance volume, margin and competitiveness.
**Tools/data:** Pricing tools, competitor price scrapes, elasticity models, sales and cost data.

- `MONITOR` How do our prices compare with competitors by product and channel, and where are we out of position?
- `FORECAST` What happens to volume and margin if we change price on this item or category?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why did margin fall (price investment, cost increase, discounts, mix)?
- `DECIDE` Should we pass on a cost increase or tariff to customers, absorb it, or change the product?
- `RESEARCH` What price moves and promotions are competitors making?

## 78. Promotions / campaign planner
**Focus:** Build the promotions calendar and make sure stock and logistics are ready.
**Tools/data:** Promo calendar, forecasts, stock and supply plans, marketing plans, funding agreements.

- `MONITOR` Is stock for upcoming promotions confirmed and in the right locations?
- `FORECAST` What volume will this promotion drive and what supply is required?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why did a past promotion over- or under-perform (stock, creative, price depth, timing, competitor)?
- `DECIDE` Which promotions give the best margin and traffic return, and which should be dropped?
- `RESEARCH` What are competitors running and what seasonal events fall in the period?

## 79. Loss prevention officer
**Focus:** Reduce theft and shrink in stores, DCs and online.
**Tools/data:** Incident and CCTV logs, exception-based reporting, shrink reports, POS data, case management.

- `MONITOR` Where and what are the highest shrink and theft hotspots by store, product and time?
- `DIAGNOSE` Are patterns suggesting organised retail crime, internal theft, supplier fraud or process failure?
- `DECIDE` What controls (tagging, layout, staffing, security, system rules) are most cost-effective?
- `FORECAST` What shrink will we see at year-end and what would intervention save?
- `RESEARCH` What are current theft trends, laws and police initiatives in our locations?

## 80. Franchise / international market manager
**Focus:** Adapt the range, supply and operations to local markets and partners.
**Tools/data:** Market performance data, partner reports, local regulations, import and tax data.

- `MONITOR` How is each market or franchise partner performing on sales, margin, stock and compliance?
- `DECIDE` Which products, sizes, prices and pack formats suit this market?
- `RESEARCH` What local regulations, tariffs, import rules and consumer preferences apply?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why is stock slow or out of stock in this country (customs, lead time, forecast, partner ordering)?
- `FORECAST` What are growth and investment requirements for entering or expanding in this market?
