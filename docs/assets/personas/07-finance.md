# 07 · Finance

Personas 61–70. Questions each role needs to answer to do their job.

**Question types:** `MONITOR` what is happening now · `DIAGNOSE` why it happened · `FORECAST` what will happen · `DECIDE` what to do · `RESEARCH` external or reference lookup

---

## 61. Financial controller
**Focus:** Accurate month-end close, reporting and control.
**Tools/data:** ERP/GL, consolidation tools, reconciliations, audit schedules, management reports.

- `MONITOR` Which reconciliations, accruals and intercompany balances are open or unexplained at close?
- `DIAGNOSE` What explains the variance between actual and budget or last year by line, entity and driver?
- `DECIDE` How should this unusual transaction be accounted for under the relevant standards?
- `RESEARCH` What have accounting standards or tax rules changed that affect our reporting?
- `MONITOR` Are our controls operating, and where are the exceptions?

## 62. FP&A analyst
**Focus:** Budgets, forecasts and scenario modelling to guide decisions.
**Tools/data:** Planning/BI tools, P&L and cash flow models, sales and cost drivers, spreadsheets.

- `FORECAST` What is the full-year outlook for revenue, margin and EBIT versus budget, and what are the risks and opportunities?
- `DIAGNOSE` What drove the change in gross margin (mix, price, cost, freight, shrink, markdown)?
- `DECIDE` What is the financial impact and return of this proposed initiative or investment?
- `FORECAST` How do outcomes change under demand, FX, tariff or freight cost scenarios?
- `RESEARCH` How do our margins and growth compare with peers and market expectations?

## 63. Supply chain finance analyst
**Focus:** Understand cost-to-serve and landed cost across the network.
**Tools/data:** Cost allocation models, logistics invoices, ERP cost data, TMS/WMS data, activity-based costing.

- `MONITOR` What is the full cost-to-serve by channel, region, customer and product?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why did the landed cost of product X increase (freight, duty, FX, handling, supplier price)?
- `DECIDE` Which channels, SKUs or customers are unprofitable after supply chain costs, and what should change?
- `FORECAST` What will the cost impact be of a network or carrier change?
- `RESEARCH` How do our logistics cost ratios compare with industry benchmarks?

## 64. Accounts payable clerk
**Focus:** Pay the right supplier the right amount on time.
**Tools/data:** AP system, three-way match (PO, receipt, invoice), supplier statements, payment runs.

- `MONITOR` Which invoices are unmatched, blocked or approaching payment deadlines or early-payment discount dates?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why does the invoice not match the PO or goods receipt (price, quantity, tax, duplicate, missing GRN)?
- `DECIDE` Do I pay, hold or escalate this invoice, and who needs to approve?
- `DIAGNOSE` Does this invoice or change in bank details look fraudulent or duplicated?
- `RESEARCH` What are the contracted payment terms and tax treatment for this supplier and country?

## 65. Accounts receivable / credit controller
**Focus:** Collect cash from wholesale and B2B customers; manage credit risk.
**Tools/data:** AR ledger, credit agency data, customer contracts, dispute logs, aging reports.

- `MONITOR` Which customers are overdue, by how much and for how long?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why is this invoice disputed or unpaid (pricing, short delivery, damage, admin)?
- `DECIDE` Should we extend, reduce or stop credit for this customer?
- `RESEARCH` What is the customer's financial health, payment behaviour and news?
- `FORECAST` What cash will we collect in the next 4–8 weeks, and what bad-debt provision is needed?

## 66. Treasury / FX analyst
**Focus:** Manage currency exposure, liquidity and hedging.
**Tools/data:** Treasury management system, FX rates, forecasts of purchases and sales by currency, bank data.

- `MONITOR` What are our net exposures by currency and by month, and what is the hedge ratio?
- `FORECAST` What is the P&L and cash impact of FX moves on purchases, sales and inventory?
- `DECIDE` What hedging instruments, tenors and ratios should we use?
- `RESEARCH` What are market views, central bank decisions and risks to key currencies we trade?
- `MONITOR` What is our cash position and liquidity headroom over the next 13 weeks?

## 67. Tax and duty specialist
**Focus:** Optimise and comply with transfer pricing, VAT and customs duty.
**Tools/data:** Tax systems, intercompany agreements, trade data, tax authority guidance, advisers.

- `DECIDE` How should intercompany goods and services be priced and documented to meet transfer pricing rules?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why does the VAT/GST return differ from expectation, and where is the exposure?
- `FORECAST` What is the duty and tax impact of changes in tariffs, origin or flows?
- `RESEARCH` What are the latest tax rules, rulings and incentives in each country we operate in?
- `DECIDE` Can duty drawback, bonded warehousing or first-sale valuation reduce our costs?

## 68. Internal auditor
**Focus:** Test controls, stock valuation and fraud risk.
**Tools/data:** Audit management tools, ERP data, process documentation, interviews, analytics.

- `DIAGNOSE` Are there anomalies in transactions (duplicates, split POs, unusual vendors, weekend postings) that indicate control failure or fraud?
- `MONITOR` Are stock counts, valuations and provisions reliable at sites sampled?
- `DECIDE` What are the highest-risk areas to audit this year and what scope is needed?
- `RESEARCH` What good-practice controls and regulatory expectations apply to this process?
- `MONITOR` Are previous audit actions closed and effective?

## 69. Inventory accountant
**Focus:** Value stock correctly; manage provisions, shrink and write-offs.
**Tools/data:** ERP inventory valuation, ageing reports, count results, cost updates, provision models.

- `MONITOR` What is inventory value by category, age and location, and how does it reconcile to the ledger?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why did stock value or margin move unexpectedly (cost updates, FX, adjustments, shrink)?
- `DECIDE` What provision is needed for slow-moving, obsolete or damaged stock?
- `FORECAST` What will year-end shrink and write-offs be at current trends?
- `RESEARCH` What accounting rules govern stock valuation and net realisable value for our situation?

## 70. CFO / finance director
**Focus:** Capital allocation, performance, risk and investor communication.
**Tools/data:** Board packs, forecasts, investor data, treasury, strategy models.

- `MONITOR` How is the business performing against guidance and where are the biggest surprises?
- `DECIDE` Where should capital go (new DC, automation, stock investment, new markets, M&A) for the best return?
- `FORECAST` What do downside and upside scenarios mean for covenants, cash and dividends?
- `RESEARCH` What are investors, analysts and rating agencies asking, and how do peers compare?
- `DIAGNOSE` What are the structural drivers of margin pressure and how fixable are they?
