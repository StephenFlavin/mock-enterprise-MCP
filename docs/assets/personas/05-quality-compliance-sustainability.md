# 05 · Quality, Compliance and Sustainability

Personas 42–50. Questions each role needs to answer to do their job.

**Question types:** `MONITOR` what is happening now · `DIAGNOSE` why it happened · `FORECAST` what will happen · `DECIDE` what to do · `RESEARCH` external or reference lookup

---

## 42. QA technologist
**Focus:** Make sure products meet specification; find root causes of failures.
**Tools/data:** Lab test reports, inspection results, product specs, defect logs, supplier QA records.

- `MONITOR` What are the pass/fail rates and defect types by product, supplier and factory?
- `DIAGNOSE` What is the root cause of this failure (material, process, design tolerance, supplier change)?
- `DECIDE` Should this batch be released, reworked, held or rejected?
- `RESEARCH` What test standards and thresholds apply to this product in each market?
- `FORECAST` Which suppliers or products are at highest risk of quality problems next season?

## 43. Factory auditor
**Focus:** Audit social and technical compliance; make sure findings are corrected.
**Tools/data:** Audit platform, checklists, previous audit reports, corrective action plans, worker interview records.

- `MONITOR` Which factories are due for audit and which have open critical findings?
- `DIAGNOSE` Are findings isolated or systemic (e.g. recurring overtime or wage record issues)?
- `DECIDE` Does this factory pass, require corrective action, or need suspension of orders?
- `RESEARCH` What are local labour laws, minimum wage and working hours rules, and recent news about this factory or region?
- `MONITOR` Are corrective actions closed on time and verified?

## 44. Product safety / regulatory specialist
**Focus:** Ensure products meet chemical, safety and labelling rules in every market.
**Tools/data:** Regulatory databases, test reports, labelling templates, product composition data, substance lists.

- `RESEARCH` What are the labelling, chemical and safety requirements for this product in each destination market?
- `DECIDE` Does this product or material comply, and what evidence do we hold?
- `MONITOR` Which regulations are changing and which products and suppliers are affected?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why did this product fail a restricted-substance test and where in the supply chain did it enter?
- `FORECAST` What will new regulations (e.g. PFAS, battery, packaging rules) cost us and when must we act?

## 45. Sustainability manager
**Focus:** Measure and reduce environmental footprint including Scope 3.
**Tools/data:** Carbon accounting tools, supplier emissions data, energy and transport data, targets, LCA data.

- `MONITOR` What is our carbon footprint by scope, category, product line and supplier?
- `DIAGNOSE` What are the largest emission hotspots in the supply chain and what drives them?
- `DECIDE` Which reduction levers (materials, transport mode, energy, supplier engagement) give the most reduction per pound spent?
- `FORECAST` Will we meet our targets on current trajectory and what is the gap?
- `RESEARCH` What do current standards, methodologies and peer commitments say about reporting and targets?

## 46. Ethical trade manager
**Focus:** Reduce modern slavery and human rights risk in the supply chain.
**Tools/data:** Supplier mapping data, risk indices, audit and grievance data, NGO reports, due diligence platforms.

- `MONITOR` Which suppliers, tiers and regions carry the highest human rights risk?
- `DIAGNOSE` What does this audit, worker grievance or media allegation indicate and how serious is it?
- `DECIDE` Do we remediate, suspend or exit a supplier, and how do we protect workers?
- `RESEARCH` What are the latest laws, forced labour import bans and sector reports affecting our regions and materials?
- `MONITOR` Do we know our supply chain beyond tier 1, and where are the gaps?

## 47. Recall/incident manager
**Focus:** Respond fast to safety incidents; trace affected product and manage communications.
**Tools/data:** Traceability data (batch/lot), sales and store stock data, regulator contacts, comms templates.

- `DIAGNOSE` Which batches, suppliers, factories and dates are affected by this issue?
- `MONITOR` Where is the affected stock now (warehouses, in transit, stores, with customers)?
- `DECIDE` Is this a withdrawal or a recall, and what do regulators require us to notify and by when?
- `FORECAST` What is the likely cost, reputational impact and customer volume of the recall?
- `RESEARCH` What have regulators and competitors done in similar cases?

## 48. ESG reporting analyst
**Focus:** Collect data and produce accurate sustainability disclosures.
**Tools/data:** ESG data platform, supplier questionnaires, finance and operations data, reporting frameworks.

- `MONITOR` Which ESG data points are missing, late or of low quality by source?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why do this year's figures differ from last year (scope change, method, real change)?
- `RESEARCH` What do CSRD, ISSB, SEC and other frameworks require us to disclose, and what has changed?
- `DECIDE` Which metrics and boundaries should we report, and how do we evidence them for assurance?
- `FORECAST` What data and systems will we need for upcoming reporting requirements?

## 49. Circular economy lead
**Focus:** Build take-back, resale, repair and recycled material programmes.
**Tools/data:** Returns and resale data, recycler and partner data, material flow data, LCA data.

- `MONITOR` How much product is collected, reused, repaired or recycled and what is the value recovered?
- `DECIDE` Which products and materials are best suited to take-back, resale or recycling?
- `RESEARCH` What technologies and partners exist for recycling our materials at scale?
- `FORECAST` What are the cost, volume and carbon benefits of expanding the programme?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why is customer participation or product recoverability lower than expected?

## 50. Supply chain risk analyst
**Focus:** Identify and quantify risks to supply: geopolitical, weather, supplier financial, cyber.
**Tools/data:** Risk monitoring platforms, supplier maps, news feeds, financial risk scores, scenario tools.

- `MONITOR` Which suppliers, ports and lanes are exposed to current events (conflict, storms, strikes, sanctions)?
- `FORECAST` What is the likely impact on supply and revenue if this region or supplier fails for 4/8/12 weeks?
- `DECIDE` What mitigations (dual source, buffer stock, rerouting, contract changes) are worth the cost?
- `RESEARCH` What is the financial health and credit outlook of our critical suppliers?
- `DIAGNOSE` Where are single points of failure in our supply map?
