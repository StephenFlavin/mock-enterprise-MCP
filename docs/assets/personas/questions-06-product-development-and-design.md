# Questions 06 · Product Development and Design

AI-suited personas only (score 3+). Questions are written the way real users ask them: some vague, some precise; some short, some long; some narrow, some broad. Follow-ups show how a conversation could realistically continue, including clarifications, corrections and scope changes.

**Type tags:** `MONITOR` · `DIAGNOSE` · `FORECAST` · `DECIDE` · `RESEARCH`
**Shape tags (after the type):** specificity (`vague` / `partial` / `specific`) · scope (`narrow` / `wide`) · complexity (`simple` / `moderate` / `complex`)

---

## 51. New product developer (AI score 4)

**Q1 · MONITOR · partial · wide · moderate** Which spring launch products are at risk of missing their in-store date, and why? Chase the suppliers on the late ones.
- ↳ How much of the delay is the supplier and how much is our approvals?
- ↳ What would it cost to recover the date?
- ↳ Draft the update for the launch meeting.
- ↳ Only the ones with open sample approvals, not the whole range.

**Q2 · DECIDE · specific · narrow · complex** For the new insulated lunch box (target cost £3.20 FOB, 60,000 units), compare the three shortlisted factories on tooling cost, capability, lead time and compliance record (quotes attached: factory_quotes.zip). Tell me the risks of the lowest-cost option and what we should see in the pre-production sample before releasing the PO.
- ↳ What would the tooling cost do to the unit price at 40,000 units?
- ↳ Which factory has the best compliance record?
- ↳ Once I approve, raise the PO for the factory we pick, and ask me before it goes for approval.
- ↳ Actually, delete the draft PO for the second factory instead.

**Q3 · RESEARCH · vague · wide · simple** The buying team want to launch an insulated lunch box and keep asking what else is out there. Can you find the main competitor products, their pricing, features and the common customer complaints, tell me where there's room to differentiate, and put it in a comparison I can share?
- ↳ Which complaint is most common?
- ↳ What would a premium version look like?
- ↳ Email the comparison to the buying team.

**Q4 · DIAGNOSE · specific · narrow · simple** The backpack sample failed the zip pull strength test again. Likely causes? Raise a corrective action with the supplier.
- ↳ What does the supplier say, and does it add up?
- ↳ What changes would you request?
- ↳ Reject this sample round and ask for a resubmission.

**Q5 · FORECAST · partial · narrow · moderate** Unit cost and margin at 20k, 50k and 100k units?
- ↳ How sensitive is it to material price changes?
- ↳ What's the break-even volume?
- ↳ Save the 50k case as a new costing version.

**Q6 · RESEARCH · vague · wide · simple** Any new materials or techniques that could cut cost or weight? Tell me which are proven in volume production and which are still experimental.
- ↳ What would they do to unit cost and compliance?
- ↳ Who supplies them?
