# 02 · Transport and Logistics

Personas 11–21. Questions each role needs to answer to do their job.

**Question types:** `MONITOR` what is happening now · `DIAGNOSE` why it happened · `FORECAST` what will happen · `DECIDE` what to do · `RESEARCH` external or reference lookup

---

## 11. Transport planner
**Focus:** Plan routes and loads; allocate carriers to hit service at lowest cost.
**Tools/data:** TMS, order and load data, carrier rate cards, route optimisation tools, tracking feeds.

- `MONITOR` Which orders and shipments need to move today and what are the delivery windows?
- `DECIDE` What is the cheapest combination of carriers, modes and load consolidation that still meets the delivery promise?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why did transport cost per unit increase this month (fill rate, mode shift, expedites, fuel surcharge, rate change)?
- `FORECAST` How much capacity will we need on each lane during peak, and do we have it contracted?
- `RESEARCH` Are there road closures, strikes, border delays or driver-hour rule changes affecting the lanes we use?

## 12. Freight forwarder / ocean freight buyer
**Focus:** Buy container capacity and manage ocean lanes at the right price and reliability.
**Tools/data:** Rate platforms, carrier contracts, booking portals, schedule reliability data, container tracking.

- `MONITOR` What are current spot and contract rates by lane compared to last month and last year?
- `DECIDE` Should we ship on contract, spot or a mix for this lane and season, and with which carrier?
- `FORECAST` Where will rates and capacity go ahead of peak season, Lunar New Year or a likely disruption?
- `DIAGNOSE` Which carriers have the worst schedule reliability or rolled bookings on our lanes?
- `RESEARCH` What are carriers' announced blank sailings, surcharges, port congestion levels and alliance changes?

## 13. Air freight coordinator
**Focus:** Expedite urgent stock by air while controlling cost.
**Tools/data:** Airline/forwarder booking systems, air waybills, PO/priority lists, cost approval workflow.

- `DECIDE` Is it worth flying this order, given lost sales if delayed vs air premium (cost per kg)?
- `MONITOR` Where is this shipment now, and will it meet the in-store or customer date?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why do we keep needing to air freight (late production, forecast error, missed booking, port delay) and which suppliers are the cause?
- `DECIDE` Can we split the shipment, combine with others, or use a partial airlift to cut cost?
- `RESEARCH` What are the current air capacity, rates and restrictions (dangerous goods, battery rules) on this route?

## 14. Customs and trade compliance specialist
**Focus:** Classify goods, minimise duty legally, and keep cross-border flows compliant.
**Tools/data:** Customs declaration systems, tariff databases, trade agreement rules, classification rulings, broker portals.

- `DECIDE` What is the correct HS/commodity code for this product, and what duty rate applies to each origin and destination?
- `RESEARCH` What tariff, quota, anti-dumping or sanctions changes affect our products and origins this quarter?
- `DECIDE` Does this product qualify for preferential duty under a trade agreement, and do we have the origin evidence?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why was this shipment held or fined (misclassification, valuation, missing licence, documentation error)?
- `FORECAST` What is the likely duty cost impact if a proposed tariff change takes effect?

## 15. Import/export documentation clerk
**Focus:** Produce accurate shipping documents so goods clear borders without delay.
**Tools/data:** Document management, ERP shipment data, e-filing portals, carrier and broker emails.

- `MONITOR` Which shipments are missing documents (commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin) with cut-off approaching?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why do the invoice, packing list and bill of lading not match, and which values or quantities differ?
- `DECIDE` Which certificates, licences or declarations does this consignment require for this destination?
- `RESEARCH` What are the document format and language requirements for importing to this country?
- `MONITOR` Which filings were rejected by customs systems and what do the error codes mean?

## 16. Last-mile delivery manager
**Focus:** Deliver to customers on time at acceptable cost; manage couriers and failed deliveries.
**Tools/data:** Courier portals and APIs, delivery tracking, customer contact data, delivery cost reports, complaints data.

- `MONITOR` What percentage of parcels are delivered on time, first attempt, by courier and region?
- `DIAGNOSE` What is driving failed deliveries and "where is my order" contacts (address quality, depot delays, courier capacity)?
- `DECIDE` Which couriers should get more or less volume by postcode, service level or product type?
- `FORECAST` What courier capacity will we need in peak, and what are the likely delay hotspots?
- `RESEARCH` What new delivery options (lockers, same-day, EV zones, low-emission rules) are available or required in key cities?

## 17. Fleet manager
**Focus:** Keep own vehicles and drivers productive, compliant and cost-effective.
**Tools/data:** Telematics, maintenance records, fuel cards, driver hours and licence records, route data.

- `MONITOR` Which vehicles are due for service, inspection or MOT, and which drivers have licence or hours-rule issues?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why are fuel costs, idle time or accident rates up for specific vehicles or drivers?
- `DECIDE` Should we replace, lease or outsource part of the fleet, and when does it pay back?
- `FORECAST` What will fleet running costs be next year including fuel, maintenance and emissions regulation?
- `RESEARCH` What regulations (low-emission zones, tachograph, driver training) apply to our operations and what is changing?

## 18. Port/drayage coordinator
**Focus:** Move containers off port quickly; avoid demurrage and detention charges.
**Tools/data:** Port community systems, terminal availability, container tracking, haulier bookings, free-time calendars.

- `MONITOR` Which containers are at port with free time expiring in the next 3 days?
- `DIAGNOSE` Why is this container stuck (customs hold, no chassis, unpaid fees, haulier delay, terminal congestion)?
- `DECIDE` Should we pay to expedite, re-route to another port, or accept the charges?
- `FORECAST` How many containers will arrive next week and do we have haulage and DC dock capacity?
- `RESEARCH` What is the current congestion, gate hours and strike situation at each port we use?

## 19. Control tower analyst
**Focus:** End-to-end visibility; spot disruptions early and trigger action.
**Tools/data:** Visibility platform, carrier and vessel tracking, ERP orders, alert dashboards, news and weather feeds.

- `MONITOR` Which in-transit shipments are at risk of arriving late, and what is the downstream impact on stock and sales?
- `DIAGNOSE` Where in the chain (supplier, origin port, vessel, destination port, DC) is the delay accumulating?
- `FORECAST` What is the predicted arrival date for each key shipment and the stockout risk from delays?
- `DECIDE` What mitigation (re-route, expedite, re-allocate stock, notify stores) is best for this disruption?
- `RESEARCH` What external events (weather, strikes, geopolitical, vessel incidents) could affect shipments in transit?

## 20. Carrier relationship manager
**Focus:** Hold carriers to SLAs; negotiate rates and improve performance.
**Tools/data:** Carrier scorecards, contracts, invoices, claims data, tender tools.

- `MONITOR` How is each carrier performing on on-time, damage, claims, invoice accuracy and responsiveness?
- `DIAGNOSE` Which carrier invoices differ from contracted rates and why (surcharges, accessorials, weight disputes)?
- `DECIDE` Which carriers should we award, retain or exit in the next tender and on what terms?
- `RESEARCH` How do our rates and service terms compare with market benchmarks and competitors' known practices?
- `FORECAST` What rate increases or capacity shortfalls should we expect from carriers next year?

## 21. Reverse logistics manager
**Focus:** Optimise the returns flow and maximise recovered value.
**Tools/data:** Returns management system, grading and disposition data, refurbishment partners, resale channel data.

- `MONITOR` What are return rates, return reasons and recovery values by category, channel and country?
- `DIAGNOSE` Which products or suppliers are driving avoidable returns and what is the cost?
- `DECIDE` What is the best disposition route (restock, refurbish, resell, donate, recycle) for each category on margin and sustainability?
- `FORECAST` How many returns should we expect after peak and what capacity is needed?
- `RESEARCH` What regulations on returned goods, extended producer responsibility or waste apply in each market?
