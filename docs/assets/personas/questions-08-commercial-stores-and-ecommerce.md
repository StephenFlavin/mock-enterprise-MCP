# Questions 08 · Commercial, Stores and E-commerce

AI-suited personas only (score 3+). Questions are written the way real users ask them: some vague, some precise; some short, some long; some narrow, some broad. Follow-ups show how a conversation could realistically continue, including clarifications, corrections and scope changes.

**Type tags:** `MONITOR` · `DIAGNOSE` · `FORECAST` · `DECIDE` · `RESEARCH`
**Shape tags (after the type):** specificity (`vague` / `partial` / `specific`) · scope (`narrow` / `wide`) · complexity (`simple` / `moderate` / `complex`)

## 71. Store manager (AI score 3)

**Q1 · DIAGNOSE · vague · narrow · moderate** Why is my shrink so high? Area keeps asking and I don't really know. Can you look at the stock adjustments for my store over the last 12 weeks, see which categories, days and staff patterns stand out, compare me with similar stores, and give me the three actions that would help most?
- ↳ Which pattern is the strongest?
- ↳ Is any of it a receiving problem?
- ↳ Raise a stock count on the worst category for Monday and add the top action to my store plan.

**Q2 · DIAGNOSE · specific · narrow · moderate** Three of my best-selling lines have gaps on shelf even though the system shows stock. What's the likely cause in our store? Raise a count on those three lines and bump them up the replenishment list.
- ↳ How much did we lose in sales?
- ↳ Is it a delivery or back-room issue?
- ↳ Actually, only the two that are in the back room, not the third.

**Q3 · DECIDE · specific · narrow · complex** Build next week's staffing plan for my store from the footfall forecast, the delivery schedule (two deliveries, Tuesday and Friday) and the promo calendar. Flag where I'm short or over by hour, assume two people are off sick on Saturday, and tell me what to cut or move first if I can't get cover.
- ↳ Which tasks can safely slip to Sunday?
- ↳ What's the minimum cover for Saturday?
- ↳ Once it looks right, approve the rota and swap Priya's Saturday shift to Sunday.
- ↳ Move Tuesday's receiving task to Dan, and remove the Saturday relabel job from the plan.

**Q4 · RESEARCH · vague · narrow · simple** Anything local that'll affect trade next month?
- ↳ Events, roadworks, competitor openings.
- ↳ How did similar things affect sales before?

**Q5 · MONITOR · partial · wide · simple** How do I compare with the other stores in my cluster on shrink, availability and labour cost? Where do I rank, and which one gap would be worth fixing first?
- ↳ Which store is the best comparator?
- ↳ Is any gap just location?

## 74. E-commerce manager (AI score 4)

**Q1 · DIAGNOSE · vague · wide · moderate** Why did conversion drop last week? It fell about 0.4 points and nobody has an explanation. Check traffic mix, availability, delivery promise, price and site errors, tell me which of those we can fix this week, and how much revenue we lost.

- ↳ Did mobile and desktop move differently?
- ↳ Roll back the delivery promise change if that's the cause, and log a ticket for the checkout errors. Ask me before changing anything on the site.

**Q2 · FORECAST · specific · wide · complex** What order volumes should we expect by day from Black Friday to Christmas Eve, and can the DC and carriers handle them? Once I've agreed the dates, set the cut-offs on site.
- ↳ What if traffic comes in 15% above forecast?
- ↳ Which carrier is the weakest link?

**Q3 · DECIDE · partial · narrow · moderate** Should we offer next-day delivery to more postcodes, given carrier capability, cost and the conversion benefit? What's the margin impact?
- ↳ Which postcodes have the best conversion lift?
- ↳ What's the carrier cost per drop?
- ↳ If we go ahead, switch on next-day for the best ten postcode areas only.

**Q4 · RESEARCH · partial · wide · moderate** What are competitors offering on delivery, returns and price for our top 20 products, and how do customers rate them? Where are we at a disadvantage?
- ↳ Where are we most disadvantaged?
- ↳ Which competitor is strongest on delivery?

**Q5 · DIAGNOSE · specific · narrow · simple** Why are partial and split deliveries up? What does it cost us? Fix the allocation rule behind it, but ask me before you change it.
- ↳ Is it stock location or allocation?
- ↳ What's the extra shipping cost?

**Q6 · DECIDE · vague · narrow · simple** How should we split scarce stock between online and stores for a hot-selling item? Think about margin, customer experience and whether we could hold some back for click-and-collect.
